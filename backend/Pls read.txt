Terminal 1: Start the FastAPI Backend
Open your first PowerShell window.

Navigate to your project directory:

PowerShell
cd "C:\Users\Asus\Desktop\e-wallet kiosk"
Activate your Python virtual environment:

PowerShell
.\venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
Fire up the Uvicorn server:

PowerShell
uvicorn app.main:app --reload --app-dir backend
Terminal 2: Start the Cloudflare Quick Tunnel
Open a second, separate PowerShell window.

Run your direct-path command to launch the temporary tunnel:

PowerShell
& "C:\Program Files (x86)\cloudflared\cloudflared.exe" tunnel --url http://localhost:8000
⚠️ Crucial Step After Restarting!
Because this is a free Quick Tunnel, Cloudflare will generate a brand-new random URL every time you launch it (for example: https://something-new.trycloudflare.com).

To make sure your payment flow doesn't get stuck, remember to do these two quick updates with the new link:

Update your .env file: Change BASE_URL to match the brand-new .trycloudflare.com link.

Update your PayMongo Webhook: Go to your PayMongo Dashboard, edit your active webhook, and paste the new URL with the /transactions/webhook path at the end
